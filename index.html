Apartment pests behave differently from villa pests. In towers they travel vertically through pipe chases and service ducts, so your neighbour's untreated kitchen becomes your problem next month. American Bite Pest Control treats apartments with methods designed for shared buildings: targeted, low-odour, and coordinated where needed.

Bed bugs in apartments

The number-one apartment pest. They arrive in luggage and used furniture, hide in mattress seams and bed frames, and feed at night. Treatment means thorough bedroom inspection, residual treatment of harbourage, hot-washing bedding, and a follow-up timed to the egg-hatch cycle. In towers, adjacent units should be checked too — bed bugs cross through wall voids.

Cockroaches in kitchens

German cockroaches spread between flats through plumbing voids. Gel baiting deep in harbourage — behind fridges, under sinks, inside cupboard hinges — beats broadcast spraying, which just scatters them. If the whole floor has them, building management should treat corridors, garbage rooms and a cluster of units together. https://bitepestcontrol.com

Ants, flies and minor invaders

Sugar ants enter via balcony doors and window frames; drain flies breed in bathroom drains. Most apartment ant problems are solved with baiting plus sealing entry points — no heavy spraying needed. Balcony gardens should be checked for nests in pots and planters.

What apartment treatment looks like
